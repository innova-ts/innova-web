> Public summarized version of* `LEG-05`*. The [Privacy Policy](./politica-de-privacidad.md) details processing through the website; this document presents our commitment and governance as an organization.

## 1. Commitment

INNOVATS processes personal data of clients, users, candidates, suppliers, and team members in accordance with Law No. 29733, its Regulations, APDP directives, and —where applicable— the European GDPR. We apply privacy by design to every new system.

## 2. Principles

Lawfulness, prior and informed consent, specified purpose, proportionality (data minimization), quality and accuracy, security, confidentiality, and demonstrated accountability. Every processing activity has a registered legal basis; without a legal basis, no processing takes place.

## 3. What Data We Process and Where

| Group | Typical Data | Systems |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| Clients and prospects | B2B contact information, company details, messages, contracts | CRM, email, billing |
| Website users | Contact information, technical data, browsing activity | Forms, analytics |
| Candidates | Professional profile, portfolio | Email / ATS |
| Team members and freelancers | Identification, contracts, payments | Internal management, accounting |
| Support | Tickets, technical logs | Help desk |

We classify all data as: public, internal, confidential, and restricted, with proportional controls for each level.

## 4. Governance

- **Management (CEO):** approves the policy and allocates resources.

- **Data and Security Officer (CTO):** oversees compliance, handles incidents and audits. Contact: privacidad@innovats.dev.

- **Processing Owners:** each area manager is responsible for their respective databases.

- Internal quarterly review and annual audit.

## 5. Rights and How to Exercise Them

We recognize the rights of access, rectification, cancellation, objection, portability, and withdrawal of consent. Please write to **privacidad@innovats.dev** (subject: “ARCO” + identity + request): acknowledgment within 2 business days and response within 10 business days. If we act as data processors (processing *your* users' data within your project), we will forward your request to you as the data controller within 24 hours.

## 6. Processors and Transfers

Our providers (cloud, email, CRM) act only under written instructions and a data processing agreement. International transfers (e.g., to the U.S.) are carried out with contractual safeguards; transfers to the EU use Standard Contractual Clauses. The current list of sub-processors is available upon request.

## 7. Retention and Deletion

Inquiries: 2 years · proposals: 3 years · tax documents: 5 years · logs: 1 year · CVs of unsuccessful candidates: 6 months. Upon expiration, data is securely deleted (NIST standard) with a deletion record; data subject to legal retention requirements is kept blocked and not used.

## 8. Security and Incidents

Encryption, MFA, least privilege, secrets management, tested backups, and access logging. In the event of a breach, loss, or unauthorized access: containment within hours, notification to the affected party and the APDP within 72 hours when applicable, and a corrective action plan with follow-up.

## 9. Training, Auditing, and Sanctions

Mandatory training upon joining and annually thereafter (including simulated phishing exercises). Quarterly access audits and an annual comprehensive audit. Internal violations: minor (retraining), serious (suspension and contractual measures), and very serious (termination and legal action).

## 10. Validity and Contact

Annual review or review following legal changes or relevant incidents. Questions: privacidad@innovats.dev · Complaints may be submitted to the APDP (www.gob.pe/minjus).
