## 7. How to Contract

1. **Request:** you submit a form or email describing your needs.
2. **Evaluation:** we assess feasibility (3–7 business days with complete information).
3. **Proposal:** you receive an estimated scope, schedule, and price, valid for 15 calendar days.
4. **Quotation and Adjustments:** comments and requested adjustments are addressed within the validity period.
5. **Contract/SOW:** the final scope, milestones, and payments are formally agreed upon and signed.
6. **Deposit:** upon receipt of the first payment, the team is reserved and the kick-off is scheduled (within up to 3 business days).
7. **Start:** formal project launch with a project board and schedule.

## 8. Prices and Payments

- **Currency:** USD by default; PEN if agreed otherwise. Prices are **plus VAT (18%)** for clients in Peru; exports of services may qualify for 0% VAT according to applicable law.
- **Standard payment schedule:** 30% deposit + 40% at an intermediate milestone + 30% upon final delivery. Recurring projects (support) are billed in advance per period.
- **Methods:** bank transfer, Wise, Payoneer, or other agreed methods. Card or PayPal payments may include a surcharge of up to 3%.
- **Late payment:** 1.5% per month on overdue balances. If payment is more than 7 days overdue, INNOVA may pause the project until payment is received, with the schedule being shifted accordingly.
- **Additional expenses:** third-party licenses, cloud infrastructure, and app stores are billed at cost + 10% management fee, unless otherwise agreed.
