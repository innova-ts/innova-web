## 1. Access and Availability

Access is free and requires an up-to-date browser with JavaScript and cookies enabled for forms. We strive for 99% availability, but the site may be interrupted due to maintenance, updates, or technical failures without giving rise to any right to compensation: this is an informational website, not a service covered by an SLA.

## 2. Permitted Use

Viewing content, sharing original links, contacting us through official forms, and using private areas with your credentials. Please cite our content with its source and link.

## 3. Prohibited Use

- Attempting to gain unauthorized access, compromise security, or exploit vulnerabilities (if you find one, report it to seguridad@innovats.dev; do not exploit it).

- Distributing malware, phishing, or spam, including malicious files through forms.

- Abusive scraping that degrades the service.

- Fraud, impersonation of INNOVATS or third parties (e.g., fake emails requesting payments).

- Submitting false information or third-party data without consent.

These activities may result in access suspension, reports to providers/authorities, and legal action (Law No. 30096).

## 4. Accounts and Forms

If you use a private area: provide truthful information, store your password in a password manager, enable MFA when available, and do not share your session. You are responsible for activity performed through your account until you report that it has been compromised. In forms, submit only your own data or data belonging to someone who has authorized you to provide it; we process such data according to the [Privacy Policy](./politica-de-privacidad.md).

## 5. Content and Intellectual Property

Information on the site may be updated without notice and does not constitute a binding offer (the offer is the proposal/SOW). Texts, the INNOVATS brand, logo, design, and code belong to INNOVA or its licensors: do not copy, modify, or use them commercially without written authorization.

## 6. External Links and User Security

We may link to third-party websites without controlling or endorsing them; review them at your own discretion. Keep your equipment up to date, do not share sessions, and verify that all payments or credentials are coordinated only through `@innovats.dev`.

## 7. Suspension, Changes, and Contact

We may restrict access in response to prohibited use or security risks, proportionally and with notice whenever possible. We will publish changes here along with their effective date. Contact: contacto@innovats.dev · Security: seguridad@innovats.dev · Governing law: Peru.
