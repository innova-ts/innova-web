## 4. Who We Share Your Data With

Only with providers necessary to operate our services: cloud hosting (AWS), email and CRM (e.g., HubSpot), demo scheduling, and anti-spam tools — all under data protection agreements. Some operate in the U.S.; for the EU, we apply Standard Contractual Clauses. We never sell your data. We only disclose it to authorities when required by a valid legal order.

## 5. How We Protect Your Data

Encryption in transit and at rest, access controls with MFA and least privilege, backups with restoration testing, monitoring, and personnel bound by confidentiality obligations. No system is infallible: in the event of a breach affecting you, we will notify you and report it to the APDP as required by law.

## 6. Your Rights

Access, rectification, erasure, objection, data portability, and withdrawal of consent. We do not make decisions with legal effects based solely on automated processing.

**How to exercise your rights:** write to **privacy@innovats.dev** with the subject line “ARCO”, your name, a copy of your identity document, and the details of your request. We will acknowledge receipt within 2 business days and respond within 10 business days. You may also file a complaint with the National Authority for the Protection of Personal Data (APDP – MINJUSDH, www.gob.pe/minjus).

## 7. Minors

The website is intended for individuals over 18 years of age. If we detect data belonging to children under 14, we will delete it and notify their legal guardian.

## 8. Cookies

We use essential cookies (functionality), preference cookies, and —only with your consent— analytics and marketing cookies. You can manage or disable them through your browser; without essential cookies, some features may not function properly.

## 9. Changes and Contact

We will publish any changes here along with their effective date. Previous versions are available at privacy@innovats.dev.
